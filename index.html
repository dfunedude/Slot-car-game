BASEMENT GRAND PRIX HO slot racing · 4 × 8 ft table

Racing in the basement

Mouse, trackpad and touch all work the same way.

Drive
Hold the trigger strip on your controller and slide along it to squeeze harder. Let go and the controller brakes. Keys: Space or ↑ for the first player, W for the second, P for the third and Z for the fourth.
Corners
The corner meter shows how hard the tyres are working. In the yellow the tail steps out; hold it in the red and the guide pops out of the slot and the car flies off, maybe off the table.
Reset
A car that has come off waits for you: tap it, press Reset on its controller, or press R, and it's set back in its slot. The computer's marshal looks after its own car.
Race
Give each lane a player, a computer driver (Rookie, Pro or Ace) or nobody. Pick practice or a race and press Start. Races wait for three red lights and go on the green. The lane swap and the crossing are where cars can meet; the computers give way to each other there.
Track
Choose two-lane or four-lane pieces, then tap a piece to add it at the glowing open end, or tap any ring to build from that end. Drag pieces and their ends snap together. Two-lane and four-lane track don't join. Rotate, Delete, Undo and ready-made circuits are on the second row. Laps are counted on the start/finish piece, and it decides how many lanes race.
Garage
Choose a lane, then a car and its paint. Each car has its own top speed, pickup and grip.
View
Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, two-finger drag or right-drag to pan. The camera button rides along behind your car.
